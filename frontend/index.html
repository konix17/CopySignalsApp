Auto-trader

A trend-following bot for BTC and ETH, tested on every day since 2018. It checks once a day and trades your demo money.

Long/short test

Each day a model ranks the 25 most traded coins. The best-scored fifth is bought and the worst-scored fifth is shorted, with paper money, futures fees and funding, so the result doesn’t depend on the market going up. It’s a live check of a backtest before any real money.

How it works

What each part does, the logic behind it, and what the tests on past data say about it.

Auto-trader (trend bot)

BTC and ETH each get half the account. Each half is split in three: one third is invested while the price is above its 50-day average, one for the 100-day and one for the 150-day; below an average, that third sits in cash (USDT). When a coin’s futures funding rate turns negative, at least a third is kept anyway.

It checks once a day at 00:05 UTC with your OKX fee on every trade. Tested on every day since 2018: +55% a year vs +35% for holding BTC, with about half the worst drop. It wins by losing less in crashes, not by beating BTC in booms.

Long/short test

Coins behave differently from each other in ways that repeat: calm coins tend to beat wild ones, coins that just fell tend to beat ones that just rose, and coins with many futures bets relative to their trading tend to do better. A model learns 42 such signals from 8 years of data (677 coins, delisted ones included) and ranks the most traded coins every day.

Buying the best fifth and shorting the worst fifth made money in every year since 2020 in a walk-forward test, also on 2024–2026 data it never saw, after futures fees and funding. It needs futures to short, so it runs with paper money until live results match the backtest.

What was tested and dropped

Copying traders (Hyperliquid, GMX, OKX lead traders), recently profitable traders, the crowd of profitable traders, dip buying, pump and breakout rules, leverage and daily market timing. Each looked good on some past data and failed on newer data, mostly because a rising market made any buying look smart.

The research scripts are in the research folder.

Real money

Nothing here trades your money. The portfolio page reads your OKX account with a read-only key. Before either strategy trades real money it needs months of paper results that match its backtest, an OKX key that can trade but never withdraw, a money limit and a stop-everything switch.

Data

OKX (prices, live stream, daily candles for the trend bot, your account), Binance (daily spot and futures candles with buying pressure, funding rates), Bybit (open interest) and Deribit (BTC implied volatility). All public; the long/short data updates once a day after the 00:00 UTC close.

Backtests show what rules would have done, not what they will do. This is a research tool, not financial advice. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
