How it works

What each part does, the logic behind it, and what the tests on past data say about it.

Auto-trader

BTC and ETH each get half the account. Each half is split in three: one third is invested while the price is above its 50-day average, one for the 100-day and one for the 150-day; below an average, that third sits in cash (USDT). When a coin’s futures funding rate turns negative (traders are paying to bet against it), the bot keeps at least a third of that coin anyway: crowded bets against a coin tend to get squeezed.

It checks once a day at 00:05 UTC with your OKX fee on every trade. Tested on every day since 2018: +55% a year vs +35% for holding BTC, with about half the worst drop. Checking every hour instead made less (+39%) because of fees.

Picks

1. Smart traders are buying (required): at least 3 consistently profitable Hyperliquid or GMX traders hold it long, most of them agree, they aren’t selling, and the price isn’t far past their entry. 2. Uptrend: above its 50-day average and up over 30 days. 3. Not overcrowded: Binance’s top futures traders aren’t all long and funding isn’t high.

3 of 3 = Strong, 2 of 3 = Good. The followed traders’ positions are re-read every minute, so a new buy shows up within about a minute. Only coins you can buy on OKX spot with enough volume are shown.

Rising now

Every 15 seconds: smaller coins up 2% in 15 minutes and 3% in the hour, on 3× their normal volume, still near the high and not already up 40% on the day. 5-minute candles tell a starting pump from one that’s topping or dumping.

Tested on 6 months and 237 coins, including ones that later died, these signals didn’t beat buying at random once fees are paid, and neither did 50 other pump, breakout and dump rules. So they’re shown and paper-tracked, but not auto-traded by default.

Selling and sizing

A pick’s stop-loss depends on how much the coin normally moves (3–20%), the target is twice as far, and the amount is set so hitting the stop costs about 1.25% of your money (0.75% for Good picks), never more than 10% in one coin or 40% in total.

A trade is sold at the stop, the target, when most of the traders leave or sell after you bought, when the uptrend breaks, or when the hold time is over. Open trades are re-priced every 2 seconds.

Demo and real money

Demo (switch in the top bar): pretend money at real prices. Demo buys and automatic demo trading follow each pick’s plan by themselves. The auto-trader has its own demo account.

Real: your OKX account, read with a read-only key. The app gives advice and alerts; you place orders on OKX yourself. Nothing trades real money.

Data

Hyperliquid and GMX (public trader positions), OKX (prices, live price stream, your account), Binance and OKX futures statistics (crowding, funding). Leaderboards and crowding refresh every 10 minutes; positions, prices, picks and accounts every minute.

Everything is on OKX’s spot market, which is licensed in the EU. The app only reacts to public data and never joins pump groups.
