How it works

What each part does, the logic behind it, and what the tests on past data say about it.

Auto-trader

BTC and ETH each get half the account. Each half is split in three: one third is invested while the price is above its 50-day average, one for the 100-day and one for the 150-day; below an average, that third sits in cash (USDT). When a coin’s futures funding rate turns negative (traders are paying to bet against it), the bot keeps at least a third of that coin anyway: crowded bets against a coin tend to get squeezed.

It checks once a day at 00:05 UTC with your OKX fee on every trade. Tested on every day since 2018: +55% a year vs +35% for holding BTC, with about half the worst drop. Checking every hour instead made less (+39%) because of fees.

Swing copies

The app follows consistently profitable Hyperliquid and GMX traders and re-reads their positions every minute. When one of them has held a long for 12 hours without cutting it in half, and the coin trades enough on OKX spot, it becomes a swing copy: one per coin, following the best-scored trader holding it.

It sells when that trader closes or halves the position, with a 25% safety stop and a 30-day limit. Each copy uses 5% of your money, half that while BTC is below its 50-day average.

Why these two

Copying every trade a trader makes barely beat the fees (+0.28% a trade), and trades they closed within an hour lost 0.59% each. Positions they keep for 12 hours made +1.33% a trade after fees: the ones they keep are the ones they’re right about.

Other strategies were dropped: coins starting to rise, 52 pump, breakout and dump rules, a top-20 coin trend portfolio and the week’s top gainers lost money or lost to holding BTC after fees, and buying what several traders hold had no test showing it would make money.

Demo and real money

Demo (switch in the top bar): pretend money at real prices. Demo buys and automatic demo trading follow each copy’s plan by themselves. The auto-trader has its own demo account.

Real: your OKX account, read with a read-only key. The app gives advice and alerts; you place orders on OKX yourself. Nothing trades real money.

Data

Hyperliquid and GMX (public trader positions), OKX (prices, live price stream, daily candles for the bot, your account) and Binance futures funding rates (for the bot). Leaderboards refresh every 10 minutes; positions, prices, swing copies and accounts every minute.

Everything is on OKX’s spot market, which is licensed in the EU. The app only reacts to public data and never joins pump groups.

Copy Signals shows what currently profitable traders hold. Past profit does not predict future profit. This is a research tool, not financial advice. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
